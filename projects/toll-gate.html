Automated Toll Gate

A sensor-triggered gate system that detects an approaching vehicle and opens automatically, then closes itself five seconds after the car has passed through.

Sensing →

UV Sensor

A UV sensor watches the gate's approach zone and detects a vehicle the moment it breaks the sensing range — this is the sole trigger for the whole sequence.

Actuation →

Servo Motor — 90°

A servo motor acts as the gate arm, constrained to rotate exactly 90 degrees between closed and open — enough to lift the barrier clear without overshooting.

Timing Logic →

5-Second Auto-Close

Once the UV sensor confirms the vehicle has passed, the Arduino holds the gate open for a fixed 5-second window before commanding the servo back to its closed position.

Circuit →

The Arduino, UV sensor, and servo motor are wired together on a breadboard — a fully prototyped build with no custom PCB.
